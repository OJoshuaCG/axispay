# ADR-0063: Currency conversion for Mexican cards (Phase 6 subset): tenant fixed rate, Banxico FIX, payer confirmation and the tenant payment settings

- **Status:** Proposed. Requested by the integration with pbx-payments (2026-10-06): its priced USD total must be converted by cirox with a fixed rate of 20 (12.30 USD x 20 = 246.00 MXN). It needs the owner's acceptance. Implements the part of Phase 6 that the integration needs and amends [ADR-0048](0048-payment-links-api-phase-3.md) (rules 3 and 4 of link creation, the 60-day maximum expiration, the settings screen).
- **Date:** 2026-10-06
- **Source:** master plan section 13 (13.1 to 13.7), 8.2, 8.4, 11.3, 10.5, 10.6, ADR-009, ADR-010; [ADR-0050](0050-linear-payment-flow-authorize-validate-capture.md); the `cirox-payment-links` change (spec B4 and B5, tasks CRX-6, CRX-7 and CRX-8; owner decisions of 2026-10-06: the conversion is a tenant-level configuration, fixed rate or Banxico, and the link expiration is at most 60 days with a default per tenant).

## Context

Stripe accounts in Mexico can only charge Mexican cards in Mexican pesos (ADR-009), but links are in USD. Until now the platform refused every `fx` request (`fx_not_available`) and the conversion never happened: a USD link paid with a Mexican card on a Mexican account was left to Stripe, which refuses it. Only the link carried a fixed rate (`payment_links.fx_fixed_rate`); the tenant had `fx.conversion_enabled`, `fx.default_mode`, `fx.markup_bps` and `fx.quote_validity_minutes` stored but nothing edited them and there was no tenant-level fixed rate. pbx-payments needs its tenant to convert the priced USD total with a fixed rate it controls, and later maybe with Banxico.

## Decision

1. **When a payment is converted** (`ConversionPolicy`, plan 13.2): the link is in USD, the connected account is in Mexico and the card was issued in Mexico. Then, if the tenant has conversion on and the link did not opt out (`fx.mode` is not `none`), the payment is converted to MXN; otherwise the checkout refuses before anything reaches the gateway (`conversion_unavailable`, the payer is told the merchant cannot charge this amount in USD to Mexican cards, and a `checkout.conversion_unavailable` audit entry is kept, at most once per link and hour). Any other combination is charged as it is. The rule is the plan's and was kept as written; it needs the account country (`gateway_connections.country`), so an account whose country is unknown never converts.
2. **Two modes.** `fixed`: the link's rate, else the tenant's `fx.fixed_rate` (`DECIMAL(18,6)`); the tenant markup is NOT applied on a fixed rate (plan 13.4) and the quote does not expire while the link is open. `banxico_fix`: the newest stored FIX that is not flagged for review, with the tenant markup (`effective_rate = round_half_up(rate x (1 + bps / 10000), 6)`), valid for `fx.quote_validity_minutes`. In both: `converted = round_half_up(original_minor x effective_rate)`, rounded once (`FxConverter`). The worked example of the pbx tenant: 12.30 USD x 20 = 246.00 MXN.
3. **Stored data.** `exchange_rates` (platform-level; unique per source, currencies and publication date; `requires_review` flag) and `fx_quotes` (per tenant and mode, immutable: the model refuses updates and deletes, and `payment_attempts.fx_quote_id` is now a composite foreign key to it). The attempt keeps `original_amount_minor` and `original_currency` (the link's) next to the charged `amount_minor` and `currency`.
4. **Banxico.** `FetchBanxicoFixJob` reads the SIE API (series `SF43718` by default, to be confirmed in Banxico's documentation) with the token in `BANXICO_SIE_TOKEN`, a 10-second timeout and 3 tries with backoff; it is scheduled on weekdays at 12:30, 13:30 and 17:00 Mexico City time, with a fallback at 09:00 the next day; it stores each publication date once (idempotent) with the raw payload and does nothing without the token. A FIX that differs more than 10 % from the previous one is stored `requires_review`, never used, and the superadmins are told. **The checkout never calls Banxico**, it only reads the table. If the newest usable FIX is more than 4 calendar days old (Mexico City dates), `banxico_fix` conversions are blocked and the superadmins are told once a day; a stale rate is never charged. Links with a fixed rate keep working.
5. **Payer confirmation (plan 13.5).** After the card's country is read and before any charge, the checkout answers `requires_currency_confirmation` with the quote: the original amount, the exact MXN amount, the rate and its source (and the date for Banxico), the merchant markup when there is one, and a button "Pay $X MXN". Nothing is charged and no attempt exists until the payer presses it; the next request carries the quote id and a literal `currency_confirmed: true`, and only a quote of that very link that converts its amount counts. An expired quote is replaced: if the amount did not change the confirmation stands, if it did the payer is asked again. The page shows an FX legend under the total ("If you pay with a card issued in Mexico, you will be charged $X MXN") computed as an estimate, without storing anything.
6. **What is reported.** The attempt, the payment events (`payment.*`, `payment_link.paid`), `GET /v1/payments` and the pre-payment validation body carry the charged `amount` and `currency` (MXN) and an `fx` block: `applied`, `mode` (`fixed` or `banxico_fix`), `source` (`merchant` or `banxico_fix`), `rate`, `rate_date`, `markup_bps`, `effective_rate`, `original_amount` and `original_currency`. It is `null` when nothing was converted (the validation body says `{"applied": false}`). The integrator must reconcile with both amounts.
7. **Link creation.** The `fx_not_available` refusal for supported modes is lifted: it remains only for MXN links and for tenants with conversion off. Omitting `fx` gives a USD link of a tenant with conversion on the tenant's default mode. A converting link must have a usable rate and reach the MXN minimum: `fx_rate_invalid` when a fixed rate is missing or more than 30 % away from the latest stored FIX (skipped while none is stored), and `amount_below_minimum_after_conversion` when the amount, converted with the fixed rate or the latest usable FIX and the tenant markup, is below MXN 10.00 (skipped for `banxico_fix` while no FIX is stored; the checkout checks again with the quote it makes).
8. **Tenant payment settings.** A "Payment settings" page in the tenant panel (`settings:manage`, owner and admin; audited as `tenant.payment_settings_updated`) edits `fx.conversion_enabled`, the mode, the fixed rate, the markup (0 to 1,000 bps) and the quote validity (5 to 120 minutes), and the link expiration default and maximum. The rest of the settings document is kept.
9. **Link expiration maximum 90 to 60 days** (`axispay.limits.max_expiration_hours`: 2160 to 1440). A tenant maximum or default stored above it is clamped when the settings are read; links already created keep their expiry. ADR-0048 is amended accordingly.
10. **Sandbox.** The sandbox cards are issued in Mexico (so a USD link of a Mexican account asks for the conversion), plus a `foreign` scenario issued in the US that is charged in USD.

## Consequences

- A USD link paid with a Mexican card on a Mexican account is no longer left to Stripe: it is converted, or refused with a clear message when the merchant cannot convert. Tenants that never enable conversion see the refusal instead of a Stripe decline (that decline would have counted against the merchant's account).
- The rate is the merchant's risk (ADR-010). A fixed rate of 20 reproduces pbx's glossary rule except for the rounding of cents: the priced USD total is converted once instead of recomputing the MXN fee, usually in the company's favour (10 USD top-up, 12.30 total: 246.00 MXN against 245.75).
- The Stripe Elements deferred intent must be updated to the MXN amount and currency before the confirmation token is created; the page script does it, and the real behavior against Stripe is one more item of the Phase 4 acceptance gate (ADR-0051), which has not been run.
- Not covered here: the `fx.mode` of a link in the panel's link form, conversion-unavailable events in the panel (it is in the audit log), and the reports grouped by charged currency (plan 13.7). They stay for the rest of Phase 6.
